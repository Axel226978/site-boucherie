About us

Three brothers, one butcher's shop, Rue du Commerce in Ciney

Our story

We learned from our parents

It all started in Erpent, in our parents' butcher's shop. Between the counter and the workshop, they taught us the trade: butchery, charcuterie, catering. And vegetable carving and wine, too.

Over 20 years later, all three of us are in Ciney, on Rue du Commerce. Some people still know the shop by its old name: “Chez Olivier et Sébastien”.

The counter changes with the seasons. We fill it with what we love to make.
