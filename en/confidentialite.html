Privacy policy

In short

This website has no form, no customer account, no advertising cookies and no analytics tool. We do not collect any data about you through this website.

Data controller

Boucherie des Frères Paul, Rue du Commerce 11, 5590 Ciney, Belgium. Phone: 083 23 11 56. Email: [to be completed].

What happens when you visit the website

  • Hosting: our host, Vercel, automatically records technical logs (IP address, page requested, date) to run and protect the website. They are kept for a short time.
  • Fonts and animations: the fonts come from Google Fonts, the animation and 3D scripts from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. To send them to you, these services receive your IP address.
  • Map: the contact page shows an OpenStreetMap map, which also receives your IP address.
  • Google reviews: the “Read the reviews on Google” button opens Google Maps. Google's policy applies as soon as you click.
  • Browser memory: the website remembers in your browser (local storage) that you have already seen the opening animation, so it doesn't play again. Nothing is sent. You can clear it by deleting the site data in your browser.

Some of these providers are based outside the European Union (United States). They commit to complying with the GDPR, in particular through the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

When you call us

If you order by phone, we write down your name, your number and your order, only to prepare it. This information is never sold or shared.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, by calling us or in the shop. If you believe your rights are not respected, you can file a complaint with the Data Protection Authority, Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels.
